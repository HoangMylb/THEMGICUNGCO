## Design Specificity Verdict

The current source has a recognisable THÈM system: yellow-first surfaces, dark-sauce contrast, explicit bánh tráng cuốn trứng lòng đào, editorial menu numbering, and one sauce language that changes role by section. It is no longer interchangeable with a neutral F&B template. Without the logo it would plausibly read as a young Vietnamese snack brand, though real food photography is still doing too little because both hero and signature assets are marked as placeholders.

The deterministic scan is clean: `detect.mjs --json app` returned `[]`. Browser evidence was unavailable: the local starter cannot launch because `vinext` and other packages remain incomplete after terminal DNS failures fetching npm tarballs. No visual overlay was injected.

## Overall Impression

The composition has matured from a graphic concept into a coherent brand system. The single biggest opportunity is no longer more visual styling: it is replacing the central placeholder images with genuine, tactile product proof and ensuring the exploration rows actually explore.

## What's Working

- Yellow is now a true identifier through hero and store bookends, with cream and dark sauce creating recovery space rather than an exhausting fast-food field.
- The Dòng sốt system has a disciplined purpose: orbit, quiet edge, product frame, functional underline, then destination route.
- The page rhythm is concise and intentional: loud → quiet → loud → functional → quiet → strong close.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Placeholder imagery still occupies both appetite-critical moments

**Source-confident:** hero and signature images are explicitly tagged as V1 placeholders. **Render-dependent:** their exact crop and appetite impact cannot be judged without a browser.

**Why it matters:** the brief asks for product proof before decoration. Until the yolk, translucent rice paper, sauce and topping texture are real THÈM images, the site cannot reliably create craving or earn a premium food response.

**Fix:** replace the two core assets first with distinct approved macro shots; use a third, different editorial image for Story rather than reusing the hero asset.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable polish homepage` only after assets and local preview are available.

### [P1] Menu category rows promise exploration but self-link

**Source-confident.** Every generated `.menu-item` points to `#menu`, its own section.

**Why it matters:** this breaks the stated craving → Menu journey at the exact point users choose a category, and it weakens otherwise excellent editorial navigation.

**Fix:** wire each row to its representative Menu destination when that route exists; until then, present them as non-interactive taxonomy or provide one honest full-Menu destination.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable harden homepage`.

### [P1] Mobile removes global navigation without a replacement control

**Source-confident:** `.site-header nav` is `display:none` below 760px and no mobile navigation control exists.

**Why it matters:** the first CTA still reaches Menu, but users arriving in later sections cannot directly reach Stores or Story without scrolling. This makes the secondary store journey less efficient on the most important device context.

**Fix:** once preview is available, validate whether a minimal mobile navigation access point is needed; preserve the hero's one-CTA hierarchy rather than adding a competing hero action.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt homepage`.

### [P2] The sauce system is structurally coherent but must be proved as food, not outline

**Source-confident:** the red treatment is reused as an orbit, edge stroke, food frame, menu underline and route. **Render-dependent:** whether its weight/gloss reads as sauce rather than generic red geometry.

**Why it matters:** this is the device carrying ownability. If it reads as a shape rather than flavour, it competes with the food it should lead toward.

**Fix:** do not add motifs; inspect the existing five uses against real macro imagery and tune only the weakest one.

**Suggested command:** no design change until render.

### [P2] Hidden direction-contract documentation contradicts the yellow-first approval

**Source-confident:** the invisible contract in `app/layout.tsx` still describes yellow as a controlled accent, although the approved system makes it primary.

**Why it matters:** it will mislead future implementation or automation even though visitors cannot see it.

**Fix:** update only the hidden design contract after runtime stability is restored; do not change the rendered palette.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable document`.

## Persona Red Flags

- **Social-first snack seeker:** sees the correct menu CTA, but cannot be turned into a craving response until the central images become real, identifiable THÈM food photography.
- **Nearby repeat customer on mobile:** can eventually reach Stores, but loses direct global access after the mobile header removes navigation.
- **Menu explorer:** can recognise every category, but clicking one receives no category-level continuation because the row self-links.

## Minor Observations

- `.orbit-two` has an animation-delay declaration but no matching element.
- The hero object's noninteractive `aria-label` may be redundant beside the descriptive image alt text.
- The absolute header is appropriate for the first composition, but there is no persistent navigation after the hero.

## Questions to Consider

- Can the sauce line be validated against real sauce pools and yolk before any visual refinement resumes?
- When Menu routes become real, should the first category lead directly to the trứng lòng đào signature rather than a generic category index?
- Is a compact mobile access point to Stores justified, or is a deliberate scroll-to-store journey part of the intended narrative?
